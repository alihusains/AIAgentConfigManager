# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Assuming good faith and asking before escalating

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind
- Trolling, personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer by opening an
issue marked "conduct" or by contacting the repository owner directly through GitHub.
All reports will be reviewed promptly and treated confidentially.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code of conduct in good faith may be
removed from the project.
